The zigzag on the left fills as you scroll, and the pulsing dot rides along the path. The purple stroke grows from the top to the bottom of the track in sync with your position on the page.

Section 1 — How it works

The path length is measured once with getTotalLength(). On every scroll event, we set stroke-dashoffset so the stroke reveals from start to current position, and use getPointAtLength() to place the marker at the tip.

Section 2 — The pulse

The marker pulses by animating its r attribute, not transform: scale(). SVG transform-origin is unreliable across browsers, but r always grows around the circle's own center.

Section 3 — Keep going

Each section adds more scrollable distance. The progress is computed from scrollY / (scrollHeight - innerHeight), so adding or removing sections doesn't break the indicator.

Section 4 — Almost there

By now the marker should be near the bottom of the track. One more section and the path will be fully drawn.

Section 5 — End

You've reached the bottom. The whole path is filled and the marker sits at the very end of the zigzag. Scroll back up to watch it reverse.
